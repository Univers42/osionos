## Archived

This note is kept for history only. It describes a February position that no longer holds,
and the paint line it refers to has since been rebuilt.

## Attendees

Ben, Chloe, the workshop foreman, the logistics planner and the service technician.
Twenty-five minutes, in the Vejle meeting room.

## Positions at the time

- The paint line was two weeks behind, because the extraction filter had failed twice.
- Timber delivery from the sawmill was arriving in weekly batches of unpredictable size.
- The service technician had eleven open warranty visits, all older than three weeks.
- Two projects were waiting for a paint slot with no date attached to them, and one of them
  turned out to be a client who had been told a date in good faith.

### The three things that hurt

The filter was the visible problem and it was not the expensive one. The expensive one was
the timber, which arrived in batches too small to cut and too large to store, and which
sat in the aisle between goods-in and the saw shop. The second was the service technician,
who had eleven open visits and a car full of parts, and who was losing a day a week to
collection instead of to repairs.

## What was decided

- Weekly capacity lists instead of monthly ones.
- A standing stock of the three most-used sheet goods.
- The service technician stops carrying parts in the car and takes them from the warehouse.
- A second extraction filter was ordered, with a delivery date of eleven weeks.

### What we would do differently now

The stock policy we took in February was a compromise: three sheet goods and a box of fittings was
not enough, and holding more was not affordable with the space we had. The warehouse system that
replaced it in April holds a much smaller buffer, but it is placed where the service technician can
reach it, which was the real problem. The extraction filter order was right and it took eleven weeks
to arrive, which is a lead time we now plan around instead of against.

## Why it is archived

The filter arrived, the line caught up, and the stock policy was replaced by a proper
warehouse system in April. The numbers in this note are historical and should not be quoted
to a client.
