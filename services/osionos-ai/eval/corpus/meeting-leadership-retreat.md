## Setting

One full day, the conference room at the Copenhagen showroom. Ana, Chloe, Ben, the
workshop foreman, the logistics planner and the two founders. Nine hours, one dinner, no
minutes read aloud.

## The clinic programme

The first item was the Arcway programme, twenty-six examination rooms in Hellerup, and the
question was whether to hold the price for twelve months as the client's procurement
department asked. <!-- answer:q31 -->

The answer was no. A price lock on a healthcare job means holding the timber price, the
labour rate and the surcharge list for a year, and one of the three will move. The
alternative was to offer the lock on the catalogue items only and to price the worktop
upgrade as an option, which keeps the decision with the client.

## The museum

The museum opening is public and the date cannot move. The concern is not the furniture, it
is the archive store: the collection moves in July and the humidity report arrived after we
quoted. The room agreed that the plinth heights are recalculated before anything is cut.

### The Lyon studio

The Lyon studio has eight people and does sales administration as well as design, which cannot
continue. The studio lead wants a commercial contact in French, full time, and the workshop cannot
commit to a start before September. Until then the studio carries the French accounts and the drawings,
and the team is stretched but it holds.

## Hiring

Two people start in the workshop and one in the Lyon studio. The Lyon studio is still
running sales admin as well as design, which is not sustainable, and the studio lead is
looking for a full-time commercial contact in French.

## Money

- The discount ladder stays as it is until the September review.
- Installation stays a separate line on every quote.
- A small materials price list is published to the sales team, updated monthly, so nobody
  argues from memory.

## What was not discussed

Remuneration was deliberately left out of the day. It is handled separately, with the
compensation bands, and the archived version of that page is the only place the numbers
are written down.

## Agreed actions

- Ana writes to Arcway procurement with the option structure.
- Ben gets the humidity report and recalculates the plinths.
- Chloe opens the Lyon studio position.
- The next retreat is in November, and the agenda is set by the workshop, not by sales.
