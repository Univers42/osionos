## What it is

The Vesper is a modular sofa for hotel lobbies, reception areas and office landings. It is
built from modules that clip into a hidden rail, so a run can be extended, shortened or
rearranged on site without a carpenter.

The NW-VESPER-12 configuration seats four and is built from six modules, two of which are
corner pieces. <!-- answer:q03 -->

## The module family

| Module | Width | Depth | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Armless | 60 cm | 90 cm | Middle of a run |
| Corner | 90 cm | 90 cm | Turn of ninety degrees |
| Arm | 75 cm | 90 cm | End of a run |
| Ottoman | 90 cm | 60 cm | Footrest or extra seat |

A run is a list of modules and two rails. The rail length is what is actually ordered, and
the modules are cut to it. That is why the drawing always shows the rail, not the sofa.

## Inside

- Frame: kiln-dried beech, corner blocks screwed and glued.
- Suspension: webbing and a foam layer, no springs, because springs fail loudly.
- Cushions: foam wrapped in a down-and-feather outer, removable for cleaning.
- Back cushions: filled separately, so the seat depth does not change with use.
- The frame is screwed from below and stays visible under the cover.

## Cover and fabric

The cover is removable, washable at thirty degrees and replaceable. The contract fabrics
are a contract wool, a heavy plain weave and a coated option for clinics. Colours are held
for ten years, which means the fabric is ordered in small quantities and stored.

## Options

- Two seat heights, forty-two and forty-four centimetres.
- A loose back panel in a second colour, at no extra cost.
- A fire-retardant foam upgrade, which is required in most hotel rooms.
- A reinforced base for public use, recommended above four seats in a run.

### Why the rail is the ordered piece

The order is for the rail and the module list, not for a sofa. The rail is cut to the length of the
run, the modules are clipped onto it, and a run that turns a corner needs a corner module. This is why
the drawing always shows the rail and the modules separately: the room is measured first, the rail is
cut to the room, and the modules follow the rail.

## What we tell clients

- The sofa arrives on the rail, so the floor must be clear on the delivery day.
- It is carried in by two people and the modules are clipped together in the room.
- The cover can be removed and cleaned, but not machine-washed above thirty degrees.
- The rail is the part that matters for the layout, and it is fixed once the room is
  measured.

## Known limits

A run that is built along a curved wall will need a corner module that we do not make.
This is the most common reason a run is re-drawn, and it is visible on the survey.
