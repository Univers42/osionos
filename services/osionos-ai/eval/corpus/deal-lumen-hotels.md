## Opportunity

Lumen Hotels is a four-property group that renovates one property a year. This year the
turn is the Copenhagen harbour property, forty guest rooms over three floors, plus a small
breakfast room on the ground floor.

The signed scope covers forty guest rooms and the breakfast area, at a value of four hundred
and eighty thousand euros. <!-- answer:q30 -->

## Who we deal with

- **Cecilie Rask**, development manager, owns the programme.
- **Johan Fenger**, general manager at the property, cares about the noise during the
  works.
- **Tine Toft**, head of procurement, signs and checks the payment plan.
- **Maja Ellgaard**, interior designer, has drawn the scheme twice already.

Maja's drawings are good and complete, which is rare. Cecilie is the one to watch: she
collected three quotations and told us openly that we were the slowest to respond.

## Progress

The quotation went out on 3 April. We are in negotiation. Cecilie asked for a single
delivery instead of two phases, and for the works to happen in July while the building is
quiet, which are the same request and cannot both be satisfied.

The workshop can build the forty rooms in five weeks. Installation needs four days with a
team of four, plus a night for the floor protection. We have offered a single delivery in
the last week of July, which is inside their quiet period but after their ideal date.

## Commercials

Cecilie wants nine per cent off the room furniture and a credit note of five per cent
against the final invoice. Our standard discount on a project of this size is five per
cent. We have offered seven per cent on the beds and bedside tables only, because those
are catalogue items, and kept the built-in wardrobe at list.

The claim that the breakfast room must be installed in the same week as the rooms has not
been priced separately yet. It should be.

### What is not in the price

The quote does not cover the removal of the old furniture, the floor protection, the electrical work
in the rooms, the evening lift access, or the making good of the corridors after the installers have been
through. It does not cover the breakfast room, which is priced separately because the works are
different and the crew is different.

## Risks

- The building has one goods lift and the client has not confirmed access hours.
- The client wants the old furniture removed and has not accepted the removal price.
- Johan is worried about the drilling noise in occupied floors, and the first floor is
  directly above the breakfast room.

## Next steps

- Price the breakfast room as a separate line so the argument moves to the rooms.
- Ask Cecilie for the lift access window in writing, with dates.
- Offer a low-noise fixing method for the first floor and price it as an option.
