## Opportunity

Quadrum is a software company moving its fourth floor into a new building. The fit-out
covers thirty-two desks, four meeting rooms of eight people, a phone room, a kitchen and a
small library corner. It is a straightforward workplace project and it is the one I run
myself, because it is small and it is near the workshop.

The total fit-out value of the Quadrum project is two hundred and sixty thousand euros,
including the kitchen and the acoustic screens. <!-- answer:q45,q46 -->

## Who we deal with

- **Sofie Ramberg**, office manager, runs the project and the move.
- **Kasper Lyng**, co-founder, signs, and has strong opinions about the meeting rooms.
- **Thea Nordin**, facilities contractor, owns the building services.

Kasper wanted a large teak table in the middle of the floor. Sofie wanted quiet. They
disagreed in our first meeting and we were asked to arbitrate, which we did by putting the
table in the kitchen and giving the floor two rooms instead of three.

## Status

Discovery. The site survey is on 29 May, the programme is drafted, and the budget is
approved internally. Nothing has been priced yet beyond the rough figure above.

## What we need from the workshop

- Desk runs for thirty-two people, in four clusters of eight.
- Four meeting rooms with acoustic screens and a power module per seat.
- A kitchen with a six-metre run, a dishwasher and a pantry wall.
- A library corner with a low shelf and four stools.
- Phone rooms, two, glazed on three sides.

### The programme

The move is planned for the second week of September, with the new floor handed over on the first.
The desks and the meeting rooms go in on the two floors above in the week after, and the kitchen last,
because the kitchen needs the electricity to be live. Sofie wants the library corner in the quiet corner
by the window, and we have drawn it there.

## Risk

The building is still being fitted out by Thea's teams. If the raised floor comes up
different from the survey, the desk runs change. We will do the survey before the quote and
build the quote from the survey, not from the floor plan.

## Next steps

- Measure everything on 29 May, including the ceiling voids for the cabling.
- Send the acoustic screen samples to Kasper, who has already rejected two.
- Ask Thea for the raised floor build-up in writing.
