## Archived

This page holds the 2026 bands. It is kept for history because the 2026 cycle is closed and
the bands are not being changed. A new cycle replaces it with a new page rather than editing
this one, so that the numbers we actually applied stay visible.

## How the bands work

Every role has a band with four steps and a midpoint. The step is decided by evidence, not
by negotiation, and the evidence is written down before the conversation.

The workshop band runs from forty-six thousand euros for a joiner to fifty-eight thousand
euros for a foreman, reviewed each April. <!-- answer:q49,q50 -->

## How a step is decided

- The scope of the work, written as the tasks the person actually does.
- The number of people supervised, if any.
- The evidence of the last two review cycles.
- Whether the person works in the workshop, in a studio or on site.

A person may be above the midpoint of their band. The band is a range for the role, not a
ceiling for the individual, and it says nothing about what anyone should be paid.

### Ce qui n'est pas dans une bande

Une bande ne dit rien du mérite, et elle ne dit rien non plus de ce que vaut le travail réellement
fait. Elle sert à payer de la même façon des rôles différents, à ce que la grille soit publique à
l'intérieur de l'entreprise, et à ce qu'une négociation individuelle parte d'un chiffre écrit plutôt que
d'une impression. Les personnes au-dessus du milieu de leur bande sont normales et courantes, et nous
ne les traitons pas différemment.

## The review cycle

Reviews happen in April for everyone, and out of cycle when a role changes. The people team
prepares the evidence, the team leader confirms the scope, and the conversation happens
with both of them present. A promotion changes the band, and a salary review alone does
not.
