## Setting

Aarhus, on site, in the room model suite rather than a meeting room. Idse Krogh, Rune
Halvorsen, Signe Bruun and their operations manager came for the afternoon. We brought the
design lead and Ben.

## What they saw

The room model was built for the review: two room types, the corner condition and the
bathroom pod, with the actual veneer samples, not photographs. Signe had rejected the
photographs, and she was right to.

## Where they pushed back

Three things came back.

- The desk in the room model has a closed pedestal, and their operations team wants an
  open one so that the cleaning contractor can reach the floor.
- The wardrobe in the model is one metre wide and they think it should be one metre twenty.
- They want the lobby reception desk finished in the same dark tone as the room joinery,
  which is a different tone from the model.

None of these are hard. All three change the drawings, so the price moves, and that is the
conversation that followed.

### The three changes, priced

None of the three changes was priced on the day, and all three are in the revised quote. Opening
the pedestal is free, because the closed pedestal was our standard and the open one costs nothing
extra. The wider wardrobe is priced as an option. The lobby desk in the darker tone is priced as a
material change, and it is the only one of the three that moves the price.

## The date

Rune asked again for the lobby to be installed before the summer season. We repeated what
we wrote in May: rooms in the second half of August, lobby in September, deposit at
signature. Idse asked for the deposit to be smaller, and we explained why it is not: the
workshop buys the timber against the deposit.

## What was decided

- The pedestal is opened, at no extra cost, and the drawing is reissued.
- The wardrobe width goes to one metre twenty, priced as an option rather than a change.
- The lobby desk tone is confirmed in a sample in June, and the price moves if the tone
  changes.
- A written programme goes out within a week with the new dates and the new line items.

## Follow-up

The client asked us to bring the same model to their Copenhagen property in July. That is
not in the price. It is quoted as a separate visit, and we agreed to send the cost.
