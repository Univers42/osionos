## Opportunity

Arcway Health Systems is building a new outpatient clinic in Hellerup. The scope is
twenty-six examination rooms, a staff area for eighteen people, a waiting hall and a small
procedure room with a higher cleaning specification.

The programme covers twenty-six examination rooms plus the staff area and is valued at
eight hundred and sixty thousand euros. <!-- answer:q31,q47,q48 -->

## The client

- **Dr. Marit Sølvberg**, medical director, signs the specification.
- **Jonas Værn Holst**, practice manager, runs the programme and the schedule.
- **Tine Dahl**, infection control, approves every surface treatment.
- **Anette Bjerg**, procurement, runs the tender process with a hard deadline.

Tine is the person who decides whether we win. She rejected our first proposal because the
worktops had a visible joint at the sink unit. She was right to.

## The specification

Every examination room is identical, which is the good news. Each needs a desk with a
cable cut-out, a wall-mounted cabinet, a stool, a low cabinet and a washable privacy
screen. Twenty-six copies of one room, plus the variations for the procedure room.

- Worktops: compact laminate with a sealed edge, not solid wood.
- Floor: vinyl in the clinical zone, carpet in the staff area.
- Screens: wipeable fabric, contract grade, no loose fibre.
- Storage: lockable, key-alike across all rooms, serviced by the client.

### Why twenty-six identical rooms

Building twenty-six identical rooms is the only way to hold the price and the schedule. Every
deviation from the standard room costs a drawing change, a longer lead time and a second visit by the
installer. The specification allows two deviations: the procedure room and the staff kitchen.
Everything else is the standard room, with the standard worktop and the standard cabinet.

## Status

The proposal went out on 30 March. We are in proposal, not negotiation. Anette has confirmed
that the tender closes on 28 August and that the award will be made the following week.

Two risks are open. First, the procedure room needs a worktop that stands up to a stronger
disinfectant, and our standard sealed edge has been queried once already. Second, the
client asked us to hold the price for twelve months, which we cannot do without the
workshop agreeing to a price lock.

## Procurement requirements

- Three years of warranty, one year longer than our standard.
- Spare parts available for ten years after delivery.
- A service contact within two hours during working days.
- A signed declaration that no timber species listed in the client's appendix will be used.

## Next steps

- Get Tine a physical sample of the sealed edge, not a photograph.
- Ask the workshop for a written quote on a twelve-month price lock.
- Confirm the ventilation model with the installer before the drawings are issued.
