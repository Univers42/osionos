## What it is

The Lark is our task chair for people who sit for eight hours. It is a plywood shell on a
cast base with a gas lift and a four-way arm, sold to offices, clinics and hotel desks. It
is the chair that wins arguments about ergonomics with a drawing rather than a certificate.

The frame kit NW-LARK-77 ships in eight weeks from the Vejle workshop, and the cast base is
delivered separately three weeks later. <!-- answer:q01 -->

The two-part delivery is deliberate. The shell is bulky and cheap to transport, the base is
heavy and awkward, and couriers handle them differently.

Three sizes, no fourth. The workshop foreman refused a fourth size in 2024 and the
production numbers have been better ever since.

## Adjustment

- Seat height from forty-two to fifty-six centimetres.
- Seat depth moves in four positions, twenty millimetres apart.
- Arm height and arm width adjust independently.
- The backrest tilts with a tension dial, not a lever.
- The seat pan tilts forward, which is the setting most used by consultants.

## Materials

- Shell: fourteen layers of moulded birch plywood, sealed with a water-based lacquer.
- Upholstery: a contract wool on the shell, or a black elastomer skin for clinic use.
- Base: cast aluminium with a nylon glider, a felt glider optional.
- Gas lift: class 4, tested to one hundred and twenty thousand cycles.

The clinic version uses the elastomer skin and a wipeable arm pad. It is not a different
product, it is the same chair with two substitutions, and it is priced accordingly.

### Why the shell is plywood and not moulded plastic

A plywood shell can be re-covered, re-sprayed and repaired, and the same shell is used for the
clinic version with a wipeable skin. Moulded plastic cannot be repaired and cannot be re-skinned, and
after five years it is a disposal problem. The plywood shell costs more and it is the reason the chair
is still in the range.

## What we tell clients

- The chair is delivered assembled apart from the base, and takes four minutes to put
  together with the supplied key.
- Every adjustment is possible by the user, without tools.
- The gas lift is replaceable by any chair shop in the country.
- Spares are available for ten years.

## Known limits

The arm pad is the part that fails first, and it is the only part on the list that is not
guaranteed for the full five years. It is a wear part, in the same way that a bicycle tyre
is.
