## Why we track this

Every quarter we collect three quotes we lost and read them properly. It is not espionage
and it is not useful: a lost quote tells us what the client wanted, not what they paid.
What we do instead is track the price bands the market accepts for the work we do.

## Price bands we see

- Hotel guest room furniture, catalogue, between six and fourteen per cent below list.
- Hotel lobbies and reception, custom, between list and eight per cent above list.
- Clinic and healthcare fit-outs, catalogue with a few custom items, close to list.
- Museum and gallery work, custom, always at or above list.
- Workplace fit-outs, catalogue, the most competitive segment we have.

## The panel supply negotiation

We buy plywood and blockboard from three suppliers. The largest of them has raised prices
twice in a year, and our volumes are not big enough to matter to them.

The panel negotiation with Eveld and Zonen, tracked internally as PEREGRINE-LOOM, asks for a
nine per cent reduction on plywood volumes above four hundred panels a month. <!-- answer:q10,q58 -->

The volume commitment is the part we have not agreed. Above four hundred panels a month we
would have to keep two lines running that we do not currently fill.

### What a competitor's price tells us

A competitor's price tells us very little on its own. It tells us the specification they priced,
and a specification is a choice somebody made. The useful question is never who is cheaper, it is what was
compared: the timber, the fixings, the steel, the finish and the erection. Two of the three quotes we
lost last quarter were not the same product as ours.

## What we do not do

- We do not publish a price list to the market. Ours is a starting point, not an offer.
- We do not match a competitor's drawing by undercutting the same specification.
- We do not bid below our cost on a one-off to enter a new segment.
- We do not discount to win a client we would rather not have for ten years.

## Where we win

We win where the specification is complicated and the programme is tight: conservation
buildings, healthcare with a real infection-control standard, and hotels where the site is
live and the noise matters. We lose where the client wants a catalogue product and the
decision is made on a spreadsheet.

## Use

Read this page before you answer a client who says another supplier was cheaper. Ask what
was compared, then answer on the specification. A client who chooses a lighter frame and a
softer timber has bought a different product and will call us when it wears out.
