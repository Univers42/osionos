## The rules that are not negotiable

The workshop runs on three rules. They are short, they are posted at every machine, and
nobody is allowed to make an exception, including the foreman.

Hearing protection is compulsory on the sanding line, and the dust extractor must run
whenever a spindle sander is switched on. <!-- answer:q17 -->

- The extractor runs before the machine starts, never after.
- A machine with a broken guard does not run, it is tagged and reported.
- Nobody clears a jam while the machine is running, including with a tool.

## Dust and extraction

Extraction is the reason the paint line is being rebuilt, and the sanding backlog is eleven
weeks long because the extraction specification was wrong for our varnish. The rules are
not superstition.

- Every machine that produces dust is connected to a filtered extraction.
- Filters are replaced on the schedule, not when the reading drops.
- The filter readings are written on the sheet by the machine, with the date.

## Training

Nobody works a machine alone before the safety induction and a supervised period on that
machine. The induction happens in person, in the workshop, and it is repeated for every new
machine, not only for new people.

- New joiners: induction in week three, per the onboarding guide.
- Moved teams: a short briefing on the new area.
- Visitors: the viewing window, and no exceptions.

### Who may work a machine

Only two groups may work a machine: the people who have been inducted on it, and the people who
are working under someone who has been inducted on it. There is no third group. An agency worker, an
intern and a contractor are all in the same category until they have been through the induction on
that machine, with that machine.

## Reporting

Any near miss is reported the same day, in one line, to the foreman. Near misses are never
blamed and never counted against the person who reported them. A report that turns out to
be unnecessary costs two minutes. A report that is missing costs a year.

## Out of hours

The workshop is empty outside core hours, and the rules change in two ways: the extractor
is switched off and the sanding line is locked. The extraction rule is the one exception
that is allowed, because running a filtered line all night costs more than it saves.

## Outside contractors

Contractors working in the workshop sign the sheet at reception, wear their own eye and
ear protection, and are inducted on the machines they use. A contractor who has not been
inducted does not touch a machine.
