## Outcome

Fjord Studio signed on 27 February and the workshop delivered in April. The project is worth
ninety-five thousand euros and it is the smallest of the 2026 room programmes, but it is the
one that worked best.

The scope was a single creative studio in Aarhus: fourteen desks, two meeting rooms of four
people each, a coffee point and a small library wall. Nothing custom except the library
wall.

## Why it worked

- The client sent drawings, a schedule and a budget in the same email, which is rare enough
  to be worth mentioning.
- The scope was small enough to finish in five weeks, so the client's own staff could plan
  around the date.
- We said no to two requests instead of pricing them at zero.

## What we said no to

The first was a curved bench along the window wall, which would have needed a template
mould and a week of jig work for a nine-thousand-euro item. The second was a matching
outdoor set for the courtyard, which the client dropped the same week they realised the
courtyard is shared with the building next door.

Both refusals were written down and both were accepted without argument. The written
refusal is worth more than the item would have been.

### What the price covered

The price covered the furniture, the delivery to Aarhus and the assembly on site. It did not cover
the removal of the old furniture, the electrical work in the ceiling void, the network cabling or the
floor protection, and the quote said so on the exclusions list. The client read the exclusions and did
not argue with them, which is what we hoped for.

## Handover

Delivery went well. The client signed the acceptance on the second of May, one day late,
because their own network cable work overran. We absorbed that without a claim, which was
the right call: the delay was not ours, and the relationship was worth more than the day.

There is one outstanding item. The library wall was installed two centimetres further from
the window than the drawing showed, because the wall was not plumb. The client accepted the
difference in writing.

## Follow-up

Fjord Studio has a second floor to fit out in 2027 and has asked us to quote early. We keep
the contact warm with a note every quarter, not with discounts.
