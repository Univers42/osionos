## Why this page is public

Clients, suppliers and visitors read this page before they come to the workshop. It is the
shortest policy we have and the only one that is not about us internally.

## Respect at work

We are a workshop with sixty people, a hierarchy and a deadline culture. Three rules carry
most of the weight.

- Nobody is shouted at, and nobody is singled out in front of the others.
- A disagreement about the work happens in the meeting, not on the floor.
- A decision is explained once, in plain words, and it is not reopened the same day.

## Visitors and suppliers

Suppliers and visitors sign the conduct sheet at reception, and nobody is shown around the
workshop floor without an escort. <!-- answer:q16,q60 -->

The escort is not hospitality. The floor is a working environment with extraction, moving
parts and a noise level that is not safe for untrained visitors. There is a viewing window
for visitors who want to see the work.

- Contractors sign the same sheet and wear the visitor badge on the left.
- Deliveries are booked into the goods-in bay and collected from there.
- Anyone on the floor without a badge is asked to leave and to sign in properly.

### What we mean by respect

The three rules are not about politeness. They are about how work gets decided in a workshop with a
deadline: a decision that has to be defended for a week is a decision that was not made properly. The
rule about one explanation is the one people fight, and it is the one that saves the most time.

## Gifts and hospitality

Gifts above two hundred euros are declared to the people team and returned if they cannot be
shared with the team. Meals are fine. Cash is never fine.

## Health, safety and the workshop

The safety rules are separate and they are not negotiable, because the law is not negotiable
either. The workshop rules are on their own page, and the induction is in person.

## Speaking up

Anyone can raise a concern with their team leader, with the people team, or with the
directors, and nobody is asked why they did not raise it earlier. Anonymous reports are
accepted through the same form as the volunteering days.

## What we ask of clients

We ask for the same three things: no shouting, one explanation of a decision, and an escort
for anyone who wants to walk through a workshop while it is running.
