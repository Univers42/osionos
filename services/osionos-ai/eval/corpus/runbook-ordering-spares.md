## What this covers

Spare parts for the catalogue range, for the projects in guarantee, and for the ten-year
promise we make to clients. This runbook says what we hold, what we order against a
programme, and how long each of them takes to arrive.

## The stock list

The stock list holds sheet goods for the three most-used products and a box of fittings
for every catalogue piece, and everything else is ordered against the programme. <!-- answer:q32 -->

Stock is not a warehouse. It is a small buffer so that a repair visit does not become a
two-week wait, and the buffer is chosen from the service log, not from a feeling.

## Fast moving fittings

Order the wall shelf fixings in packs of fifty, because the workshop loses them faster
than it uses them. <!-- answer:q24,q53 -->

- Wall shelf plate fixings: packs of fifty, six weeks.
- Gas lift, class 4, for the task chair: boxes of four, eight weeks.
- Cable tray lids for the conference table: boxes of ten, five weeks.
- Arm pads for the task chair: boxes of twenty, four weeks.
- Module clips for the sofa: boxes of forty, seven weeks.

### Why the buffer is small

A large buffer is money sitting on a shelf, and it is money that cannot buy timber. The buffer is
sized from the service log: what the technician actually asks for, how often, and how long each part
takes to arrive. When a part is used twice in a quarter it is on the list; when it is used once in two
years it is ordered against the programme, like everything else.

## Ordered against a programme

Anything that belongs to a specific project is ordered when the project is confirmed, not
before. This includes the custom tops, the conservation plinths and the clinic worktops.

- Custom parts are ordered on the day the drawing is approved.
- A project cancellation after ordering is paid for, at cost, by the project.
- The delivery date on a custom part is the date the workshop gives, in writing.

## Suppliers

- Eveld and Zonen supply sheet goods and plywood.
- The small fittings come from two suppliers in Germany and one in Poland.
- The gas lifts come from a single supplier, with a twelve-week lead time.

We keep two suppliers for everything except the gas lifts, because a single source with a
twelve-week lead time is a risk we accepted once and would not accept again.

## Damaged in transport

A part damaged in transport is replaced on the next delivery, with the photograph of the
crate, and the carrier invoice is settled by us. There is no argument about who pays for a
part that arrived broken in our own van.

## When we are out of stock

Tell the service technician the exact date, not a promise. The technician calls the client
with the date, and the client is told the same day. An early promise that slips is worse
than an honest date that holds.
