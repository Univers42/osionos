## Where it stands

Northlake Hotels Group runs eleven properties across Denmark and Norway. This opportunity
covers the complete refurbishment of eighty-four guest rooms and the ground-floor lobby of
their Aarhus flagship. It sits in negotiation, the third stage, and it is the largest single
room programme we have in the pipe for 2026.

The agreed commercial value with the Northlake Hotels Group is one million two hundred
thousand euros across eighty-four guest rooms and the lobby. <!-- answer:q27,q35 -->

## People on the client side

- **Idse Krogh**, head of rooms, signs for the hotel group.
- **Rune Halvorsen**, project manager, runs the programme and owns the calendar.
- **Signe Bruun**, interiors, approves every finish sample.
- **Poul-Erik Madsen**, finance, checks the payment milestones before signature.

Rune and Signe have been our counterparts since the first site walk. Idse only joined the
calls in March and has not yet seen a sample in person, which is the main risk on this deal.

## Timeline

- 2026-01-20 — first enquiry through the showroom, no drawings.
- 2026-02-11 — discovery meeting in Aarhus with Rune and Signe.
- 2026-03-04 — survey of four prototype rooms, measured and photographed.
- 2026-03-26 — proposal sent, eighty-four rooms plus lobby, with the standard exclusions.
- 2026-04-22 — second site visit, sample review of the oak and the upholstery.
- 2026-05-15 — client asked for a shorter installation window; we answered in writing.
- 2026-06-30 — target signature date, conditional on finance agreeing the milestones.

## The commercial argument

Northlake wants the lobby delivered before the summer season, which means installation in
June. The workshop can do that only if the deposit lands by the fourth of May, and it did
not. Our position is a first draw in the second half of August, with the lobby in September.

The hotel programme expects the first draw at signature, with the second on delivery and
the balance thirty days after handover. <!-- answer:q28 -->

The client has asked twice for a price reduction of six per cent across the room programme.
We have offered two per cent on the room furniture and kept the lobby at list, because the
lobby carries the custom reception desk and the stone-topped credenza.

### Where the schedule is stuck

The schedule is stuck in one place: the deposit. The workshop can hold a slot in the schedule for two
weeks without money, and after that the slot goes to whoever pays. We asked for the deposit on the
fourth of May, we did not get it, and the first delivery date we can hold is the second half of August.
Idse has now asked for a smaller deposit, which does not solve the problem, because the timber is
bought against the deposit and not against the schedule.

## Risks

- The client reads the deposit as a commitment to a June date, which it is not.
- Signe has not approved the Ferrolle finish in person, only from a photograph.
- We have not yet agreed who pays for the removal of the old carpet and the floor protection.

## Next steps

- Book the sample review with Idse in the second week of June.
- Ask Rune for the written confirmation of the fifty-fifty split between the two properties.
