## What it is

The Marlow is a height-adjustable desk with a single column per side, a frame that lifts
one hundred and thirty kilograms and a worktop that is either a compact laminate or solid
oak. It is sold to offices and to clinics, where the worktop has to survive a sink.

The NW-MARLOW-21 frame is rated for one hundred and twenty kilograms and lifts between
sixty-two and one hundred and twenty-eight centimetres. <!-- answer:q04 -->

## Heights and speeds

| Position | Height | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | 62 cm | Seated work |
| Standard | 74 cm | Standing work, short tasks |
| High | 92 cm | Standing work, drawing |
| Maximum | 128 cm | Bar height, counter |

The frame moves at forty millimetres per second under load and fifty-two without. The
difference is noticeable and is why the noise level is quoted as a number rather than a
word.

## The motor and the control

The motor has a twelve-week lead time from the supplier, which is the reason the control
box is not being changed this year. There are four memory positions and a counter that
shows the height in millimetres rather than a level. <!-- answer:q57 -->

- Anti-collision: the frame stops and reverses if it meets an obstruction.
- The child lock is a two-second hold on the control.
- The frame is rated for ten thousand full cycles in the first year.
- The control is replaceable without taking the frame apart.

## Worktops

| Worktop | Depth | Thickness | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compact laminate | 80 cm | 25 mm | Sealed edge, clinic use |
| Compact laminate | 60 cm | 25 mm | Small footprint |
| Solid oak | 70 cm | 32 mm | Not suitable for clinics |
| Solid oak | 90 cm | 32 mm | Double monitor width |

The sealed edge is a two-coat system with a cure time between the coats. It is slower to
produce and it is the only edge treatment we can offer for a wet room.

## Options

- A cable tray under the worktop, which is the most ordered accessory.
- A monitor arm clamped to the frame, not to the worktop, so the worktop can be replaced.
- A drawer box under the worktop, which adds thirty kilograms and needs the deep frame.
- A half-height return for a shared bench.

### Why the load rating is published

The frame is rated for one hundred and twenty kilograms, and the rating is on the drawing, on the
website and on the box. A desk that tips over in a clinic is not a warranty question, it is an injury,
and the only defence is a number that the client could check before buying. The rating covers the frame,
not the worktop: a solid oak top on a deep frame is limited by the frame, not by the timber.

## What we tell clients

- The frame is delivered assembled and the worktop is fixed on site.
- Floor protection is included for the delivery, because the frame is heavy.
- The frame is guaranteed for five years, the electrical parts for three.
- A frame that moves under load is a fault, and we replace the lift, not the whole frame.
