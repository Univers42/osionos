## Attendees

Chloe, Ana, Ben, the design lead and the workshop foreman. Fifty minutes, Vejle.

## Agenda

The council reviews the range twice a year. March is the spring round. Three items were on
the table: the conference table family, the standing desk family, and a new shelving system
for the museum work.

## Conference table family

The Halden table was designed in 2021 and is still our most requested single piece. The
decision was to keep the top in solid oak and to stop offering the stained ash version,
which accounted for nine per cent of production and most of the complaints. <!-- answer:q34 -->

The table top is built as one slab for lengths up to three metres and in two parts beyond
that. The two-part version needs a visible joint, and clients find it. We now quote the
joint honestly in the drawing set instead of hoping nobody notices.

## Standing desk family

The Marlow frame stays as it is for a year. The motor supplier has a twelve-week lead
time and we are not changing parts on a live family. <!-- answer:q22 --> A new control box was tested and rejected
because the client-side software was worse.

## New shelving system

A sealed-edge shelving system for conservation and archive rooms was approved for the
range, under the Rive Nord name, with an adjustable plinth. The first unit goes into the
museum archive in the summer.

## Decisions

- The stained ash table version leaves the range at the end of the year.
- The Rive Nord shelving system enters the range with two plinth heights only.
- The Halden top thickness stays at forty-two millimetres.
- No new product is announced before the September review.

### Why the range is shrinking

Every product in the range carries a ten-year spare parts promise, and a promise is only worth
making if the workshop can still make the part in ten years. That rules out anything that depends on a
supplier who might stop. It is also why the conference table keeps one timber and the standing desk
keeps one motor: fewer variations, fewer drawings, fewer things to get wrong on a site where nobody
has the manual.

## Note on the range

We add fewer products than we used to. Every new family carries a ten-year spare parts
promise, and that promise only holds for products we can still make in ten years.
