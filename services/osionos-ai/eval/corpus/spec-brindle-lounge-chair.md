## What it is

The Brindle is a lounge chair for hotel lobbies, waiting areas and the corner of a
boardroom. It is a bent ply shell on a turned leg frame, upholstered in one piece, and it is
meant to be sat in for an hour, not five minutes.

The NW-BRINDLE-08 shell comes in four upholstery options: wool bouclé, saddle leather,
recycled felt and a fire-resistant plain weave. <!-- answer:q05 -->

## The shell

The shell is pressed in one piece from nine layers of birch ply, which is why it has no
seam along the back. The shell is left unfinished on the underside so that moisture from a
wet coat does not stay in the veneer.

- Height at the seat: forty-three centimetres.
- Overall depth: seventy-eight centimetres.
- Seat width: fifty-six centimetres.
- Weight: nine kilograms, which a two-person lift handles without a trolley.

## The frame

The frame is solid ash, turned in our own shop, and joined with a steel dowel and glue. The
legs splay four degrees, which is what stops the chair rocking on a soft carpet. <!-- answer:q55 -->

| Part | Material | Replaceable |

| Shell | Pressed birch ply | Yes, as a shell |
| Legs | Turned ash | No, as a set |

## Upholstery

The four options are not finishes of one fabric, they are four different contracts of
behaviour.

- Wool bouclé is the default and the softest.
- Saddle leather is the most expensive and the one that changes most in colour.
- Recycled felt is the most durable and the hardest to clean.
- The fire-resistant plain weave is required in public areas and looks like nothing
  special, which is the point.

## Options

- A higher seat, four centimetres, for older users.
- A loose seat cushion in a second colour.
- A swivel base instead of the four legs.
- A blanket and an integrated reading light, as a hotel option.

### Why the shell is pressed and not bent

A pressed shell comes out of a mould in one piece, which is why it has no seam along the back and
why it keeps its shape after five years of hotel use. A bent shell would be cheaper per chair and would
fail on the back rest, usually after two years. The mould costs money and it is paid for once, when the
tool is made.

## What we tell clients

- The chair arrives in two parts and is assembled on site with the supplied key.
- The shell is replaceable on its own, which is the reason the frame is made separately.
- The leather option will darken in the first year; that is the material, not a fault.
- Spares are available for ten years, including the glides.
