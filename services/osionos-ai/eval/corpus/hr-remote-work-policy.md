## The rule

Staff may spend up to three days each week away from the building once they have completed
their first ninety days in the company. <!-- answer:q14 -->

Before the ninety days are up, remote days are a conversation with the team leader and not
a default. A probation period is not a reason to refuse the request, but it is a reason to
agree it out loud.

## How a remote day is agreed

The days spent working from home are booked a week ahead in the people system, and the plan
is visible to the whole team. <!-- answer:q33 -->

Bookings are made in the same system as leave, because a remote day and a leave day compete
for the same person on the same day. There is no separate tool and no separate rule.

- A request is made before Wednesday for the following week.
- Two consecutive remote days need the team leader's agreement in the same request.
- A whole week remote needs the people team, not only the team leader.

## What stays the same

Remote days change the place of work and nothing else. The contract, the pay, the hours,
the notice period and the working calendar are identical for everyone.

- The working day is eight hours, with the same core hours as the workshop.
- Meetings that need a room are booked as if everyone were in the building.
- Equipment is collected in person, and equipment is returned in person.
- The right to disconnect is the same in both places, including the notification rule.

## Working from another country

Working from another country needs the people team and a written note from the tax
adviser, and it is limited to twenty working days a year. The company laptop may not leave
the European Economic Area without a written agreement, and the rule is not negotiable
because it is an insurance condition.

### Equipment

Equipment is collected in person from the Copenhagen office, never shipped, and it is returned in
person when a remote arrangement ends. A laptop that travels by post is a laptop that is lost. The
equipment list is the same for everybody, and it is the same whether the week is spent at home or in
the building.

## For the workshop

The workshop is not remote and never will be. Two days a week, everyone in the workshop is
on site, because the extraction, the sanding and the fitting depend on people being in the
same room.

## If the arrangement stops working

A remote day can be withdrawn by the team leader for a specific week, with a reason, and
without notice period. Nobody has to justify wanting to come in. People who work from home
for a year and then change their mind do it without telling anyone why.
