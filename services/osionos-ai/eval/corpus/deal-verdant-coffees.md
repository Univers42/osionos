## Summary

Verdant Coffee Roasters has grown from one roastery café to three: the original site in
Nørrebro, a new one on Aarhus island and a third planned for the airport district. They
want the same furniture language in all three, which is a good fit for us and a bad fit for
a fixed budget.

The opportunity is worth three hundred and forty thousand euros for the three sites, with the
airport site still conditional on a lease that has not been signed. <!-- answer:q29 -->

## The three sites

- **Nørrebro** — twenty-two seats, a long counter and a mezzanine. Open since 2022, the
  furniture is ours already and the new work is an extension of the counter run.
- **Aarhus island** — thirty-one seats, full fit-out, signed off in principle.
- **Airport district** — eighteen seats, depends on the lease.

## What the client cares about

Hanne Sørvik, the founder, does not care about the product names. She cares about three
things and repeats them in every call.

- The counter must be built in one piece so there is no joint in the middle of the bar.
- The oak must be the same across the three sites so that a future repair matches.
- Nothing may squeak, and the stools must not rock.

The last point sounds small. It is the reason we lost the first round to a cheaper supplier:
their stools rock on the tile floor and their client complained every week.

## Where we are

We sent the proposal on 12 May after a second visit to Nørrebro. Hanne asked for a ten per
cent reduction across the three sites and a two-year payment plan instead of the standard
milestones. Both requests are still open.

The workshop can start the Aarhus island counter in the week of 17 August if the order is
confirmed by the end of July. Nørrebro is quicker, four weeks, because we can reuse the
existing carcass dimensions.

### Why we lost the first round

We lost the first round to a supplier from Poland in March, and the reason was not price. Their
stools rock on tile, their counter was made in three pieces, and Hanne told us after the fact that she
had seen the counter wobble. The second proposal answers both points in the first two drawings, and
that is what changed her answer, not the discount.

## Open questions

- Does the airport lease close before the end of 2026? If not, we invoice two sites.
- Hanne wants a fifth stool colour that is not in the standard range. It is a two-week
  powder-coat sample.
- Who removes the old counter at Nørrebro? Nobody has said it out loud yet, so we have put
  it in the exclusions.

## Next steps

- Send the counter section drawing with the single-piece joint detail.
- Price the custom stool colour as an option rather than a discount.
- Confirm the Nørrebro removal price in writing before the quote is revised.
