## Opportunity

The Brightwater Museum is opening a new wing and wants the galleries built as furniture,
not as plinths bolted to a wall. Six galleries, a reading room, a conservation store and a
public area with seating.

The museum commitment carries a value of one million four hundred and fifty thousand euros
for six galleries, the reading room and the archive store. <!-- answer:q27 -->

## The client

- **Dr. Anselm Weiß**, the director, signs and attends everything.
- **Marta Kjeldsen**, curator of the new wing, wrote the brief.
- **Bram Hoff**, the architect, produced the conservation specification.
- **Rikke Salto**, project manager, runs the programme.

Bram's conservation rules are the hard part. The archive store needs stable humidity, so
the furniture there is solid, modular and set on a raised plinth with no timber touching
the floor.

## Programme

The work splits into three parts, and the client is aware that the third part is the
expensive one.

- Gallery furniture, six rooms, mostly seating and vitrines.
- Reading room, twelve study desks and the shelving that runs the full wall.
- Archive store, shelving and handling tables with a sealed surface.

The archive shelving is the Rive Nord system, the only product in our range with a sealed
bottom edge and an adjustable plinth for the humidity floor. <!-- answer:q20 -->

### The reading room desks

Marta wants the twelve study desks to match the ones in the old wing. Those were made in 2021 and
are no longer in the catalogue, so the old drawings are the only reference we have. Copying them is
cheaper than designing new ones and it keeps the wing consistent, which is what the museum wants. We
still need a survey of the old desks before we can promise a match.

## Status

Discovery finished in March, the proposal went out on 11 May, and we are now in
negotiation. Dr. Weiß has asked for the whole programme to open in the first week of
September, with the archive store installed in July because the collection moves first.

Marta has asked us to keep the reading room desks identical to the ones in the old wing,
which were made for us in 2021 and are now out of catalogue. That is a custom job, and it is
cheaper to copy them than to design new ones.

## Commercials

The museum wants a phased payment with nothing due before the wing opens, which is the
opposite of our standard. We have offered thirty per cent on order, sixty per cent on
delivery and ten per cent thirty days after the opening, which is our usual shape moved by
one milestone.

## Risks

- The archive humidity report arrived after we quoted; if the numbers changed, the plinth
  height changes with them.
- The gallery vitrines are subcontracted, and subcontracting a conservation-critical item
  makes everyone nervous.
- The opening date is public and fixed.

## Next steps

- Get the plinth heights recalculated against the final humidity report.
- Quote the reading room desks as a copy of the 2021 pattern, with a new drawing set.
- Put the vitrine subcontractor in the contract, not in a side letter.
