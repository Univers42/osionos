## What this covers

A claim made by a client on a product we delivered, inside or outside the warranty period,
and the service visit that follows. Every claim gets a number, a name against it and a
closing note.

## What a claim needs

A claim needs three documents: the order number, the delivery note and a photograph of the
defect. <!-- answer:q36 -->

Anything else can be added later. If the photograph is missing, the technician takes one
on the visit, and the claim is not closed until it exists.

- The order number finds the contract, the milestones and the delivery date.
- The delivery note proves when the product was handed over.
- The photograph saves a second visit in half of the cases.

## Inside the warranty

Five years on structure and fixings, two years on finishes and moving parts. A claim
inside that period is free, including the travel.

- The technician attends, photographs and replaces the part on the same visit when the
  part is in the van.
- If the part is not in the van, the claim is closed with a date, not a promise.
- A repair that fails twice is escalated to the workshop and the batch is reviewed.

## Outside the warranty

A claim made after the warranty period is paid as a service visit, at the client's cost,
and it is quoted before the technician travels. <!-- answer:q25 -->

The quote is the visit fee plus the part, and it is sent the same day. If the client does
not accept it, the claim is closed with the reason.

## Escalation

- The first claim on a product is handled by the service technician.
- The second claim on the same batch goes to the workshop foreman.
- The third claim on the same product in a year goes to the quality review, with the
  batch number, and the batch is examined.
- Any claim involving a safety issue is reported the same day, in one line, to the
  foreman.

### Who the technician talks to

The technician talks to the person who raised the claim, not to the contract. If the person who
raised it has left, the claim is closed with a note and no visit, because a visit without a contact is a
visit that cannot be closed. The client is told the same day when a claim is closed without a visit,
with the reason in one line.

## Common reasons

- Loose fittings after six months, mostly on the wall shelf.
- Seams opening on a conference table top.
- Swelling near water in clinic worktops.
- A gas lift that sinks, which is a part and not a fault.

## Closing a claim

Every claim is closed with what was done, what was replaced and what the client said. The
log is read monthly by the workshop, and the three most common reasons become the agenda
of the quality review.
