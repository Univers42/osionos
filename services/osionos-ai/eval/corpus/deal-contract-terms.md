## Payment milestones

Our standard payment schedule is thirty per cent when the order is confirmed, sixty per
cent when the goods leave the workshop and ten per cent thirty days after handover. <!-- answer:q28 -->

The milestones follow the work, not the calendar. A client who asks for nothing due before
the opening is asking for a different business, and we answer it by moving one milestone,
not by moving all three.

## Delivery

The production window is given in working weeks and starts on the date the order is
confirmed, not on the date the deposit lands. Standard delivery is ten weeks for catalogue
furniture and between fourteen and twenty-two weeks for custom work. <!-- answer:q51 -->

Installation is a separate line. We do not include it in the furniture price, because the
site decides how long it takes and the site is not ours.

## Warranty

- Five years on structure, joints and fixings.
- Two years on finishes, including the smoked oak line.
- Two years on moving parts, replaced rather than repaired.
- The warranty covers the product, not the client's care of it.

Spare parts stay available for ten years after delivery. This is a contractual promise and
it is the reason we keep a small production record for every catalogue piece.

### How the schedule moves in practice

The milestone that moves in practice is the second one. A client with a slow site asks for the
balance to move to the end of the quarter, and the answer is that the balance follows the handover,
not the quarter. What we do agree is a handover date in writing, and a programme that shows who is on
site on which day.

## Acceptance

The client has ten working days to raise a defect after handover. A defect raised inside
that window is corrected before the final milestone is due. A defect raised after it is
serviced under the warranty, at the client's cost for the visit.

## Exclusions

Every contract lists what is not included. The list is never empty, and it usually contains
removal of existing furniture, floor protection, power and data, ventilation models, and
the making good of uneven walls.

## When the terms move

Only two things move without a new contract. The installation date moves when the site
moves. The payment schedule moves when the sales director and the workshop manager agree in
writing. Everything else needs a new order.
