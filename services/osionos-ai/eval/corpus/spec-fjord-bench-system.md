## What it is

The Fjord is a bench system for long walls: waiting areas, canteens, changing rooms and
corridors where people sit for a few minutes. It is a beam on legs, a plywood seat and a
back, sold by the section, with the sections bolted together along the wall.

The NW-FJORD-15 bench system is assembled from one-metre-twenty sections that bolt
together in under ten minutes. <!-- answer:q06 -->

## Sections

| Section | Length | Seats | Weight | Fixing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 120 cm | 2 | 24 kg | Floor plate |
| Double | 240 cm | 4 | 46 kg | Floor plate |
| Corner | 120 cm | 2 | 26 kg | Floor plate, side return |

A run of any length is a list of sections and a drawing. The corner section turns the run
ninety degrees without a separate piece of timber.

## How it is fixed

- Every section has its own floor plate, so a run is not one rigid object.
- The plates are screwed, not glued, and the screw heads are countersunk.
- The beam is fixed to the plates with four bolts per section.
- A run is levelled with one adjustable foot per plate, not per section.

## Materials

- Beam and legs: laminated ash, visible edges sealed.
- Seat and back: nineteen millimetre birch plywood, sealed both sides.
- Padding: a thin recycled foam on the seat only.
- Finish: water-based lacquer, matt, repairable by the client.

## Use

The system is built for short stays. It is not a sofa and it is not a lounge seat: the
back angle is upright on purpose, because a waiting area needs people to be able to stand
up without pushing a heavy chair.

- Museums: galleries and the education room.
- Clinics: corridors and the waiting hall.
- Workplaces: the canteen and the entrance hall.
- Hotels: the breakfast room and the lift lobby.

### Why the run is drawn as sections

A run is drawn as a list of sections and a rail, never as one object, because that is how it arrives
and how it is fixed. It also means a section can be replaced without taking the run apart, which
matters in a corridor where the wall is the only thing nobody wants to open. The floor plates are the
parts that fail, and they are the parts that are easy to change.

## What we tell clients

- The run is delivered section by section and bolted together in the room.
- The floor must be flat; we check the level on the survey and say so in writing.
- The seat height is fixed at forty-five centimetres.
- Spares are available for ten years, including the floor plates.

## Known limits

The system cannot follow a curved wall, and a run longer than twelve metres needs a
mid-span support that is not in the current family.
