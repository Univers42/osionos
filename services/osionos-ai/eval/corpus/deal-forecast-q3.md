## The number

Eight opportunities are tracked for the third quarter. Their combined gross value is four
million nine hundred thousand euros, of which four million eight hundred and five thousand
euros is still open after Fjord Studio closed in February.

The open pipeline for the third quarter stands at four million nine hundred thousand euros
across eight opportunities. <!-- answer:q29 -->

## Weighted view

We weight each stage: ten per cent for discovery, thirty-five per cent for proposal, sixty
per cent for negotiation and one hundred per cent once signed. On that basis the weighted
figure is two million four hundred and ninety-four thousand two hundred and fifty euros.

### How the number is built

The gross figure is the sum of the eight opportunities at their quoted value, with no deduction for
anything. The open figure removes the one that is signed. The weighted figure applies the stage factors
to the gross figure and nothing else; no risk is subtracted for a client we know is short of money,
because that judgement belongs to the weekly review and not to a spreadsheet.

## What the forecast is not

It is not a commitment and it is not a target for the workshop. The workshop plans on the
signed value and the confirmed start dates, nothing else. In 2025 the forecast was eleven
opportunities and six of them never reached a proposal; the weighted number was never worth
much on its own.

## How we use it

- The workshop manager reads the negotiation and proposal rows only.
- The monthly review looks at days since the last written contact, not at the value.
- Any row with no written request from inside the client for four weeks is removed from the
  forecast until there is one.
- The Lyon studio reads the forecast to plan its own capacity in French-speaking accounts.

## Risks we can see

Brightwater is the largest single line and its opening date is public, so a slip is visible
to everyone. Northlake depends on a finance sign-off that has not happened yet. Arcway
depends on a price lock the workshop has not priced.
