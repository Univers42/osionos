## Attendees

Ana, Ben, Chloe, the Copenhagen showroom team and the four account managers, in person in
the Vejle canteen. Notes taken by Ben, thirty-five minutes.

## What we agreed

The quarter opened with eight live opportunities and a workshop that could not build all of
them. The first discussion was about what to drop.

- The showroom team stops quoting custom reception pieces before the workshop has confirmed
  capacity in writing.
- Every opportunity that reaches proposal gets a drawing set before a price.
- No account manager sends a revised price without the discount ladder in the reply.
- The Lyon studio takes the French-speaking accounts from March, and quotes in French.

## The pipeline we discussed

The number discussed was the open pipeline, four million nine hundred thousand euros, and
the objection was that it counted the museum twice, once as galleries and once as the
archive store.

An opportunity drops out of the report when four weeks pass without a written request
from inside the client. <!-- answer:q21,q56 -->

The forecast page now shows one line per opportunity, with the galleries and the archive
store inside the same row. The weighted number was not the point of the meeting.

## The workshop capacity

Ben brought the capacity list. Eleven weeks of backlog in sanding, four weeks in assembly,
and a paint line that is booked solid until the middle of September. Nothing in the pipe is
worth doing if it cannot be built, which is why the showroom rule above exists.

## What people will do differently

- Ana writes a one-page brief before every discovery call.
- Chloe reviews the drawings of every healthcare proposal, because infection control is
  where we lose work.
- Ben publishes the capacity list every Monday morning.
- The account managers send the client a programme, not a price, when a request is vague.

### What the quarter looks like

Eight opportunities, one workshop and a paint line that is booked until September. The quarter is
not short of work, it is short of capacity, and the first job of the sales team is to stop offering work
the workshop cannot take. Ana opens every discovery call with the capacity list on the table, which is
why two enquiries went to competitors in January without anybody noticing for a month.

## Open points

Nobody could say how many opportunities are actually live, because "live" had three
definitions in the room. We agreed to use the written-contact rule and to stop arguing
about it.

Next meeting is the monthly review, second Thursday, same time.
