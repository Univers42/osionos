## What it is

The Halden is a solid oak conference table with a steel frame and a cable tray. It is
designed for eight to sixteen people and for rooms where the table is the centre of the
argument, which is most of them.

Table top NW-HALDEN-03 is a solid oak slab, forty-two millimetres thick, finished with
hardwax oil. <!-- answer:q02 -->

## Lengths and seating

| Length | Seats | Top weight | Frame | Notes |

| 4.0 m | 16 | 106 kg | Three trestles | Two joints |

A joint is shown on the drawing whenever the length passes two point eight metres. We used
to hide it. Clients find it anyway, and the ones who do not are the ones who complain later.

## The timber

The top is solid oak, and the family is kept to one timber so the drawing set does not
multiply. <!-- answer:q34 -->

- Timber: European oak, quarter-sawn where the board allows it.
- Board width: minimum one hundred and eighty millimetres across the full length.
- Moisture content at delivery: between eight and ten per cent.
- Movement: the frame is fixed to the top through a slot, so the top can breathe.

## The frame and the tray

The frame is powder-coated steel, black or a warm grey, with levelling feet that take out
the difference between an old floor and a new one. The cable tray runs the length of the
table under the middle, on the spine, with two access lids.

## Surface treatment

Two treatments are offered and both are repairable by the client.

- Clear hardwax oil, matt, refreshed every two years with a commercial pad.
- A fumed dark treatment, which is a chemical darkening of the same oak and cannot be
  repaired locally without a full re-fumage.

### Why the joint is shown

A joint above two point eight metres is drawn, priced and explained in the quotation. We hid it for
years and the clients found it on site anyway, which is worse than showing it. The joint is a solid
tongue and a spline, glued and screwed, and it is as strong as the rest of the top. The only thing that
changes is the line you can see if you look for it.

## What we tell clients

- The table is delivered in two parts for lengths above two point eight metres and needs
  two people to move.
- It must stand on a level floor; the levelling feet cover twenty millimetres.
- The top is not a work surface for hot pans, and the oil finish marks easily at first.
- Spares for the cable tray lids are available for ten years.
